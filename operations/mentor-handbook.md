# Mentor handbook

For Elixpo mentors in Google Summer of Code 2027. Every mentor also signs the
commitment published on the
[agreements page](https://gsoc.elixpo.com/agreements/) and follows Google's
[responsibilities for mentors](https://developers.google.com/open-source/gsoc/help/responsibilities).

## Primary mentor

- Plans the work with the contributor during community bonding: milestones,
  a midterm checkpoint, the weekly schedule and planned absences, written
  down and signed off by the contributor and both mentors.
- Holds the weekly one-to-one call and exchanges at least one more written
  update each week ([communication.md](communication.md)).
- Reviews every pull request personally and aims to reply within 48 hours on
  weekdays.
- Keeps the written plan up to date, including any agreed change of scope.
- Completes Google's midterm and final evaluations, after agreeing the result
  with the backup mentor ([evaluation.md](evaluation.md)).

## Backup mentor

- Knows the same codebase well enough to review and unblock.
- Reads every weekly report and joins the call at least every other week.
- Joins reviews, and takes over all primary-mentor duties during any absence,
  so that the contributor never waits for a reviewer.

## Absence cover

- Tell the contributor, the other mentor and the organisation administrators
  in advance of any absence of two working days or more.
- The backup mentor takes over the weekly call and reviews for that period.
- If both mentors are unavailable, an organisation administrator checks in
  with the contributor and arranges cover.
- Tell the administrators early if your capacity will drop for longer, so
  that cover can be arranged before it is needed.

## Review standards

- Review all work personally. Automated checks are evidence for a review,
  not a replacement for it.
- Write specific comments that explain what to change and why.
- Prefer small, reviewable pull requests merged into the main repository as
  the work progresses.
- Apply the [AI policy](https://gsoc.elixpo.com/ai-policy/): meaningful use
  of AI tools is disclosed, and the contributor must be able to explain every
  line.

## Notes for evaluations

After each weekly call, keep brief private notes:

- progress against the written plan;
- communication (calls held, reports posted, responsiveness);
- concerns raised, and what was agreed.

Evaluations are based on these notes and on the public record (reports,
issues, pull requests), not on memory. Share notes only with the other
mentor and the organisation administrators.

## Conflicts of interest

If you know an applicant personally, declare it to the organisation
administrators and step away from ranking that applicant.

## Things to escalate

Tell the organisation administrators promptly about lack of communication,
inactivity, progress well behind the plan, a possible violation of Google's
programme rules (such as plagiarism or harassment), or a conflict you cannot
resolve. See [escalation.md](escalation.md).
