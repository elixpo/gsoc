# Communication

How the contributor, mentors and organisers keep in touch during Elixpo's
Google Summer of Code 2027 programme. The public summary is at
[gsoc.elixpo.com/operations](https://gsoc.elixpo.com/operations/).

## Weekly cadence

| What | Who | When |
|---|---|---|
| One-to-one call | Contributor and primary mentor; the backup mentor joins at least every other week | Every week, at a time agreed in the written plan |
| Written update | Contributor and primary mentor | At least once more each week, so they are in contact at least twice a week (Google asks mentors for this) |
| Weekly progress report | Contributor, posted publicly | Every week, on the project's tracking issue ([template](weekly-report-template.md)) |
| Progress log | Programme coordinator | Every week, linking each report |

## Where to talk

- **Technical discussion** (design, review, defects): issues and pull
  requests in the project's repository, in public, so other maintainers can
  follow and help.
- **Private matters** (availability, personal circumstances, concerns about
  people): gsoc@elixpo.com.
- **Code of Conduct reports**: see [escalation.md](escalation.md).
- **GitHub Discussions** for GSoC 2027 will open once Google announces the
  programme dates. Until then, the programme email is the channel for
  questions.

## Response times

- Mentors aim to reply within 48 hours on weekdays, including first review
  of a pull request.
- The organisation administrators acknowledge any concern within two working
  days.

## Raising a blocker

1. Name it in the weekly report, with what you have tried.
2. Tell the primary mentor straight away; do not wait for the next call.
3. No reply within 48 hours on weekdays: write to the backup mentor.
4. Still no reply: write to gsoc@elixpo.com. See [escalation.md](escalation.md).

## Availability

Contributors and mentors record planned absences in the written plan agreed
during community bonding, and announce any new absence of two working days
or more as early as possible. See [mentor-handbook.md](mentor-handbook.md)
for how cover works.
