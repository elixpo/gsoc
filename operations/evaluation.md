# Evaluations

How Elixpo will handle Google's midterm and final evaluations in Google
Summer of Code 2027. Google's rules and dates always take precedence; see the
[programme rules](https://summerofcode.withgoogle.com/rules), the
[timeline](https://developers.google.com/open-source/gsoc/timeline) and the
mentor guide on
[evaluations](https://google.github.io/gsocguides/mentor/evaluations).

## Who completes them

- The **primary mentor** completes Google's midterm and final evaluations,
  after agreeing the result with the backup mentor.
- The **organisation administrators** track every evaluation deadline, make
  sure each evaluation is submitted at least 36 hours before Google's
  deadline, and complete it themselves under Google's process if a mentor
  cannot.
- If the mentors disagree, the organisation administrators decide.
- The **contributor** completes Google's own evaluations and, at the end,
  submits their final work.

## What they are based on

1. Progress against the milestones agreed in the written plan, as amended
   by any agreed change of scope.
2. Communication: weekly calls held, weekly reports posted, blockers raised
   promptly.
3. The code, tests and documentation merged or ready for review.

Evaluations use the mentors' weekly notes and the public record. They are
not based on the number of commits or lines of code.

## Early warning

1. **Talk early.** As soon as work falls behind the plan, the primary mentor
   raises it in conversation, without waiting for an evaluation.
2. **Put it in writing.** The mentor follows up in writing to the
   contributor, with the organisation administrators copied.
3. **Adjust scope where reasonable.** The mentors and contributor agree what
   can be dropped or deferred, and record it in the plan.
4. **Agree a recovery plan** with dated steps, signed off by the contributor,
   both mentors and an administrator.
5. **No surprises.** A contributor at risk of not passing is told so in
   advance and in writing, never through the evaluation itself. Google asks
   mentors to talk to the administrators and the contributor before any
   decision not to pass.

## Google's rules

Elixpo follows Google's rules on evaluations, including that a contributor
who does not pass an evaluation leaves the programme. Evaluation results are
shared only as Google's process allows; the content of evaluations is not
published.

## Extensions

If the plan needs more time, the contributor, mentors and an organisation
administrator may agree an extension within Google's limits. It must be
entered before Google's deadline for changes.

## Final work

Mentors help the contributor prepare a final work summary that links the
merged pull requests, tests and documentation, and lists what remains. Open
tasks are filed as issues before the programme ends.
