# After the programme

Elixpo takes part in Google Summer of Code to help one contributor become the
supported owner of one part of a real project. This is what we will do once
the final evaluations are complete.

## Before the programme ends

- Remaining work is merged, or documented with what is left to do.
- Open tasks are filed as issues and known defects are recorded, so the work
  can continue with or without the contributor.

## An invitation to stay

The contributor is invited to continue as a reviewer and maintainer of the
part of the project they built, with the same mentors available for
questions. Accepting is optional.

## Follow-up

An organisation administrator contacts the contributor about 30 days and
about 90 days after the programme ends, to ask:

- what they are working on, in Elixpo or elsewhere;
- what support would help them keep contributing;
- what we should change for the next contributor.

We record whether the contributor is still active after each follow-up, as
Google suggests organisations do, and respond to Google's surveys after the
programme.

## Credit

Elixpo publishes contribution records for attributable public work at
[elixpo.com/contributors](https://elixpo.com/contributors), each with a stable
record ID and links to its evidence. Work from the programme is credited
there on the same basis: merged changes, reviews, releases and
documentation, not commit count.

## Retrospective

After the final evaluations, Elixpo will publish an honest retrospective on
[gsoc.elixpo.com](https://gsoc.elixpo.com/): what was planned, what was
merged, what was not finished and why, and what we will change. It will not
include the contents of evaluations or anything the contributor asks to keep
private.
