# Escalation and conduct

## If something is not working

Raise it in this order, moving on when a step does not resolve it:

1. **The primary mentor.**
2. **The backup mentor.**
3. **The organisation administrators**, Ayushman Bhattacharya (lead) and
   Vivek Yadav, at gsoc@elixpo.com. They acknowledge a concern within two
   working days.
4. **Google's programme administrators**, at gsoc-support@google.com, if a
   concern cannot be resolved within Elixpo.

Contributors may go straight to the organisation administrators about a
problem with a mentor, or about a significant disagreement over their work
or plan.

## Code of Conduct

- Everyone in the programme follows the
  [Elixpo Community Code of Conduct](https://github.com/elixpo/elixpo/blob/main/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md);
  the site summary is at
  [gsoc.elixpo.com/code-of-conduct](https://gsoc.elixpo.com/code-of-conduct/).
- Reports go to gsoc@elixpo.com with the subject "Code of Conduct report".
  Do not report in public.
- Reports are handled privately by the organisation administrators.
- **Conflict of interest.** If a report or concern involves one of the
  organisation administrators, write directly to the other administrator,
  using the addresses on the
  [mentors page](https://gsoc.elixpo.com/mentors/). Anyone with a conflict
  of interest steps away from the decision.

## Google's programme rules

Violations of the
[Google Summer of Code programme rules](https://summerofcode.withgoogle.com/rules),
such as plagiarism, harassment or fraud, are escalated from mentors to the
organisation administrators, and reported by the administrators to Google,
as Google requires. The administrators also tell Google about a contributor
who withdraws.
