## Has Elixpo been accepted into Google Summer of Code 2027?

No. Elixpo is preparing to apply as a mentoring organisation. Google has not
yet announced Google Summer of Code 2027, and organisations are chosen by
Google.

## Has Elixpo taken part in Google Summer of Code before?

No. Elixpo is a first-time applicant.

## How many contributors will Elixpo mentor?

Elixpo is a first-time applicant and plans to mentor one contributor in 2027.

## How big are the projects?

90 hours (small) or 175 hours (medium). Four ideas are 175 hours and two are
90 hours. We are not offering 350-hour projects.

## How hard are the projects?

Difficulty is Easy, Intermediate or Hard. Two ideas sit between two levels:
"Easy–Intermediate" and "Intermediate–Hard". Each idea page states its level.

## Who are the mentors?

Vivek Yadav, Ayushman Bhattacharya and Divyanshu. Each idea has a primary and
a backup mentor. See [People](/people/).

## Can I propose my own idea?

Yes, but discuss it in GitHub Discussions first. It must fit one codebase
and have a mentor who can review it.

## Can I apply for more than one idea?

Google sets the rules on how many proposals you may submit. We prefer one
strong proposal backed by a real contribution to that idea's repository.

## Do I need to contribute before applying?

Yes. A small contribution to the repository for your idea is part of our
[selection criteria](/selection/).

## Can I use AI tools?

For research and learning, yes. Meaningful AI use in code must be disclosed.
AI-generated proposals are not accepted. Read the [AI policy](/ai-policy/).

## When are the deadlines?

Google has not announced the 2027 dates. See the [timeline](/timeline/).

## Who do I contact?

Ask project questions in
[GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/orgs/elixpo/discussions). For
programme questions, email [gsoc@elixpo.com](mailto:gsoc@elixpo.com). See
[Contact](/contact/).
