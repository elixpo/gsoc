## What is Google Summer of Code?

A programme run by Google in which contributors new to open source work on an
open-source project for several months, with mentors, and receive a stipend.
Mentoring organisations such as Elixpo provide the project ideas and the
mentors. See the [home page](/#what-is-google-summer-of-code) or the
[official site](https://summerofcode.withgoogle.com/).

## What is Elixpo's status for 2027?

Elixpo is applying to be a mentoring organisation for Google Summer of Code
2027. Google selects participating organisations and announces them after the
application period.

## How many contributors will Elixpo mentor?

In 2027 we plan to mentor one contributor.

## Who can apply?

Google sets eligibility, and the rules can change each year. Read them on the
[official Google Summer of Code site](https://summerofcode.withgoogle.com/).

## How large are the projects?

90 hours (small) or 175 hours (medium). Four ideas are 175 hours and two are
90 hours. We do not offer 350-hour projects.

## How difficult are the projects?

Difficulty is Easy, Intermediate or Hard, as Google defines them. One idea is
Easy, three are Intermediate and two are Hard. Each idea page states its
level.

## Who are the mentors?

Ayushman Bhattacharya, Vivek Yadav and Divyanshu. Each idea has a primary
mentor (the contributor's main contact) and a backup mentor (who can step
in). See [Mentors](/mentors/).

## Who are the organisation administrators?

Ayushman Bhattacharya (lead organisation administrator) and Vivek Yadav. They
manage Elixpo's application and its communication with Google.

## Is Elixpo selling anything through this programme?

No. No Google Summer of Code idea involves payments, pricing or paid
features, and Elixpo does not currently accept sponsorship funds. See
[Money and commercial interests](/agenda/#money-and-commercial-interests).

## Can I propose my own idea?

Yes. Describe it in an email to
[gsoc@elixpo.com](mailto:gsoc@elixpo.com) first. It must fit one codebase and
have a mentor who agrees to review it.

## Can I apply for more than one idea?

Google sets the number of proposals you may submit. We prefer one
well-researched proposal supported by a contribution to that idea's
repository.

## Do I need to contribute before applying?

Yes. A small contribution to the repository for your idea is part of our
[selection criteria](/selection/). Each idea page lists warm-up tasks, and
the [contributor guide](/contributor-guide/) describes how to begin.

## Can I use AI tools?

For research and learning, yes. If an AI tool produced a meaningful part of
your code, you must disclose it. Proposals written by AI are not accepted.
Read the [AI policy](/ai-policy/).

## Where do I submit my proposal?

You must submit your final proposal through the
[official GSoC website](https://summerofcode.withgoogle.com/). Use our
[proposal template](/proposal/).

## When are the deadlines?

Google publishes the official dates. See the [timeline](/timeline/) for the
programme stages and a link to Google's timeline.

## Whom do I contact?

Email [gsoc@elixpo.com](mailto:gsoc@elixpo.com) with questions about the
programme or an idea, and report defects as an issue in the relevant
repository. GitHub Discussions for GSoC 2027 will open once Google announces the
programme dates. See [Contact](/contact/).
