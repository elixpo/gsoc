## What is Google Summer of Code?

A programme run by Google in which people new to open source work on a real
open-source project for a few months, with a mentor, and are paid for it.
Organisations like Elixpo offer the project ideas and the mentors. Read more
on the [home page](/#what-is-google-summer-of-code) or the
[official site](https://summerofcode.withgoogle.com/).

## Has Elixpo been accepted into Google Summer of Code 2027?

No. Elixpo is preparing to apply as a mentoring organisation. Google has not
yet announced Google Summer of Code 2027, and organisations are chosen by
Google.

## Has Elixpo taken part in Google Summer of Code before?

No. Elixpo is a first-time applicant.

## How many contributors will Elixpo mentor?

Elixpo is a first-time applicant and plans to mentor one contributor in 2027.

## Who can apply?

Google sets who can apply, and the rules can change each year. Read them on
the [official Google Summer of Code site](https://summerofcode.withgoogle.com/).

## How big are the projects?

90 hours (small) or 175 hours (medium). Four ideas are 175 hours and two are
90 hours. We are not offering 350-hour projects.

## How hard are the projects?

Difficulty is Easy, Intermediate or Hard, as Google defines them. One idea is
Easy, three are Intermediate and two are Hard. Each idea page states its level.

## Who are the mentors?

Vivek Yadav, Ayushman Bhattacharya and Divyanshu. Each idea has a primary
mentor (your main contact) and a backup mentor (who can step in). See
[People](/people/).

## Can I propose my own idea?

Yes, but discuss it in GitHub Discussions first. It must fit one project
and have a mentor who can review it.

## Can I apply for more than one idea?

Google sets the rules on how many proposals you may submit. We prefer one
strong proposal backed by a real contribution to that idea's repository.

## Do I need to contribute before applying?

Yes. A small contribution to the repository for your idea is part of our
[selection criteria](/selection/). The [contributor guide](/contributor-guide/)
shows you how.

## Can I use AI tools?

For research and learning, yes. If AI did a meaningful part of your code, you
must tell us. Proposals written by AI are not accepted. Read the
[AI policy](/ai-policy/).

## When are the deadlines?

Google has not announced the 2027 dates. See the [timeline](/timeline/) for
the usual stages.

## Who do I contact?

Ask project questions in
[GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/orgs/elixpo/discussions). For
programme questions, email [gsoc@elixpo.com](mailto:gsoc@elixpo.com). See
[Contact](/contact/).
