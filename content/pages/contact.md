## Where to ask

- **Questions about an idea or the code:**
  [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/orgs/elixpo/discussions).
- **Bugs:** the issue tracker of the relevant repository.
- **Programme questions** (eligibility, process, anything private):
  [gsoc@elixpo.com](mailto:gsoc@elixpo.com).
- **Code of Conduct concerns:** follow the reporting steps in the
  [Code of Conduct](https://github.com/elixpo/elixpo/blob/main/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

We aim to reply within 48 hours on weekdays.
