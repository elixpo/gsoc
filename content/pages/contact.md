## Where to ask

- **Questions about an idea or the code:** ask in
  [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/orgs/elixpo/discussions), our
  public forum.
- **Bugs:** open an issue in the relevant repository.
- **Programme questions** (who can apply, how it works, anything private):
  email [gsoc@elixpo.com](mailto:gsoc@elixpo.com).
- **Code of Conduct concerns:** follow the reporting steps in the
  [Code of Conduct](https://github.com/elixpo/elixpo/blob/main/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

We aim to reply within 48 hours on weekdays.
