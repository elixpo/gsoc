## Reading the list

- **Size** is the expected total effort: 175 hours (medium) or 350 hours
  (large). The two 350-hour ideas each define a 175-hour core, and two
  175-hour ideas describe a 350-hour extended version; say which size you
  propose.
- **Difficulty** follows Google's levels: four ideas are Hard and three are
  Intermediate.
- **Mentors**: each idea has a primary mentor, who is the contributor's main
  contact, and a backup mentor, who knows the project and can step in.
- Each idea page lists warm-up tasks. Complete at least one before you write
  your proposal.
- The ideas describe the problem and the expected outcomes, not a solution.
  Your proposal must set out your own understanding and your own plan.

Terms are defined in the [glossary](/glossary/).

## Proposing your own idea

You may propose an idea of your own. Describe it in an email to
[gsoc@elixpo.com](mailto:gsoc@elixpo.com) before writing a proposal. It must fit one codebase and have a mentor who agrees to
review it.
