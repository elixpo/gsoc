## Sizes and difficulty

- **Size** is the expected effort: 90 hours (small) or 175 hours (medium).
  We are not offering 350-hour projects.
- **Difficulty** is Easy, Intermediate or Hard. Two ideas sit between two
  levels, and say so.
- You can suggest your own idea, but talk to us in
  [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/orgs/elixpo/discussions) first. It
  needs a mentor who can review it.
