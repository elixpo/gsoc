## How mentoring works

- Mentors are people, not bots. Every review of your work is done by a human.
- We aim to reply within 48 hours on weekdays.
- Your primary mentor is your main contact. Your backup mentor knows your
  project and can step in.
- Our programme coordinator helps with community questions, but does not
  mentor or review code.
