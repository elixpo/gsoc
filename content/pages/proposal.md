lead: Write your proposal yourself, in your own words. Short and specific is better than long and general.
---

Copy these headings into your proposal. Share a draft with us in
[GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/orgs/elixpo/discussions) before the
deadline, so we can ask questions.

## 1. About you

- Name, GitHub handle and time zone.
- Your relevant experience. Link to code you have written.
- Why this idea, and what you want to learn.

## 2. The problem in your own words

Explain the problem as you understand it after reading the code. What is
broken or missing today, and who does it affect? Don't copy the idea page.

## 3. Approach and design decisions

- How you plan to solve the problem.
- The main design decisions, the options you considered, and why you chose
  one.
- What you are unsure about, and how you will find out.

## 4. Milestones

- Break the work into milestones of one to two weeks, each ending in a pull
  request that can be reviewed.
- Mark a **midterm checkpoint**: what will be merged by then.
- Say what you will drop first if you fall behind.

## 5. Testing and proof

How will you and your mentors know the work is done? Link your plan to the
idea's "Proof of success". Include at least one test that fails before your
change and passes after it.

## 6. Communication plan

- How often you will post progress, and where (we prefer public channels).
- How you will ask for help when you are blocked.

## 7. Availability

- Hours per week you can commit.
- Exams, travel, jobs or other commitments during the programme.

## 8. Prior contributions to Elixpo

Link your pull requests, issues and discussions in Elixpo repositories. Say
what you learned from the review.
