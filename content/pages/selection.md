## What we look at

- **Understanding of the problem.** You can explain the problem in your own
  words and point to the code involved.
- **A small earlier contribution.** We look at the quality of a small
  contribution to the idea's repository, and at how you responded to review.
  Quality matters more than size or number.
- **Communication.** You ask clear questions in public, give updates and
  respond to feedback.
- **A realistic plan.** Milestones that fit the idea's size, a midterm
  checkpoint, and a testing plan that matches the idea's proof of success.

## How it works

1. We read every proposal and compare it with the criteria above.
2. We interview the applicants on our shortlist (the few strongest
   applications). We ask about your proposal and your contribution.

We never choose on the proposal alone. We do not choose on how many pull
requests you have opened.

## What leads to rejection

- A proposal written by AI. See our [AI policy](/ai-policy/).
- Made-up evidence, such as claimed contributions or results that don't
  exist.
- Breaking the [Code of Conduct](https://github.com/elixpo/elixpo/blob/main/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
