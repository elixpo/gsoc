## Criteria

- **Understanding of the problem.** You explain the problem in your own words
  and identify the code involved.
- **Prior contribution.** We assess the quality of a small contribution to
  the idea's repository and how you responded to review. Quality matters more
  than size or number.
- **Communication.** You ask clear questions, report progress and
  act on feedback.
- **A realistic plan.** Milestones that fit the idea's size, a midterm
  checkpoint, and a testing plan that matches the idea's success measures.

## Process

1. Mentors read every proposal and assess it against the criteria above.
2. Mentors interview shortlisted applicants about their proposal and their
   contribution.
3. Elixpo ranks the proposals on the official GSoC website. Google allocates
   contributor slots and announces the accepted contributors.

We do not select on the proposal alone, and we do not select on the number of
pull requests opened.

## Grounds for rejection

- A proposal written by AI. See the [AI policy](/ai-policy/).
- Fabricated evidence, such as claimed contributions or results that do not
  exist.
- A breach of the [Code of Conduct](https://github.com/elixpo/elixpo/blob/main/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
