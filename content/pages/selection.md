## What we look at

- **Understanding of the problem.** You can explain the problem in your own
  words and point to the code involved.
- **A small prior contribution.** The quality of a small contribution to the
  relevant repository, and how you responded to review. Quality matters more
  than size or count.
- **Communication.** You ask clear questions in public, give updates and
  respond to feedback.
- **A realistic plan.** Milestones that fit the idea's size, a midterm
  checkpoint, and a testing plan tied to the proof of success.

## How it works

1. We read every proposal against the criteria above.
2. We interview shortlisted applicants. We ask about your proposal and your
   contribution.

We never select on the proposal alone. We do not select on how many pull
requests you have opened.

## What leads to rejection

- A proposal written by AI. See our [AI policy](/ai-policy/).
- Fabricated evidence, such as claimed contributions or results that don't
  exist.
- Breaking the [Code of Conduct](https://github.com/elixpo/elixpo/blob/main/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
