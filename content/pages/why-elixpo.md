## Is Elixpo an established, active open-source organisation?

Yes. The Elixpo GitHub organisation was created on 24 February 2024 and
develops its projects in public. Each of the five participating repositories
had changes pushed in October 2026. See the
[Elixpo repositories](https://github.com/orgs/elixpo/repositories), the
[participating codebases](/projects/) and the [dated history](/about/).

## Is the software under an OSI-approved licence?

The source code of all five participating repositories is licensed under the
MIT License, which is OSI-approved. Each repository's current `LICENSE` file
combines the MIT terms with Elixpo's brand terms; open pull requests replace
it with the standard MIT text and move the brand rules to `TRADEMARKS.md`
([LixBlogs](https://github.com/elixpo/blogs.elixpo/pull/555),
[LixSketch](https://github.com/elixpo/sketch.elixpo/pull/209),
[Crumb](https://github.com/elixpo/crumb.elixpo/pull/251),
[Elixpo Open Source](https://github.com/elixpo/opensource.elixpo/pull/98),
[Oreo](https://github.com/elixpo/oreo/pull/61)).

## Is there an active contributor community?

Elixpo maintainers took part in Hacktoberfest in October 2024 and October
2025, preparing work for outside contributors and reviewing their pull
requests. In October 2025 the Chapter repository merged
[47 Hacktoberfest pull requests from 24 GitHub accounts](https://github.com/elixpo/elixpo_chapter/pulls?q=is%3Apr+is%3Amerged+label%3Ahacktoberfest+merged%3A2025-10-01..2025-10-31).
See the [community and mentoring record](/about/#community-and-mentoring-record).

## Who mentors, and is there enough capacity?

Three mentors, two organisation administrators (Ayushman Bhattacharya, lead,
and Vivek Yadav) and a programme coordinator (Anwesha Chakraborty). In 2027
we plan to mentor one contributor, with a primary and a backup mentor on
every idea. See the [mentors page](/mentors/).

## Are the project ideas well defined?

Each of the six ideas is one problem in one codebase, with expected
outcomes, what is out of scope, how success is measured, warm-up tasks, a
size of 90 or 175 hours, and two named mentors. Every project can be set up
from its public repository without private keys. See the
[ideas list](/ideas/).

## How do contributors get started, and how are they selected?

The [contributor guide](/contributor-guide/) sets out the steps from choosing
an idea to a first reviewed pull request and a proposal. Mentors assess every
proposal against the published [selection criteria](/selection/) and talk to
every shortlisted applicant before ranking.

## How will you communicate with and evaluate the contributor?

A weekly call with the primary mentor, at least one more written exchange
each week, and a public weekly progress report. Evaluations are based on the
agreed milestones, communication and merged work, with early written warning
if work falls behind. See [How we run the programme](/operations/).

## How will you keep the contributor involved afterwards?

The contributor is invited to continue as a reviewer and maintainer of the
part they built, with follow-up about 30 and 90 days after the programme and
credit in Elixpo's public contribution records. See
[After the programme](/operations/#after-the-programme).

## What is your policy on AI tools?

Contributors may use AI tools to learn but must understand, test and be able
to explain everything they submit, and disclose meaningful use in pull
requests. Proposals written by AI are not accepted, and mentors review all
work personally. See the [AI policy](/ai-policy/).

## Is anything in the programme commercial?

No. No Google Summer of Code idea involves payments, pricing or paid
features, and Elixpo does not currently accept sponsorship funds. Some hosted
Elixpo services offer optional paid plans; Google Summer of Code work does
not involve them. See
[Money and commercial interests](/agenda/#money-and-commercial-interests).

## What mentoring experience do the organisers have?

Elixpo's maintainers ran Hacktoberfest participation in 2024 and 2025. Ayushman
Bhattacharya is a member of GitHub's Maintainer Advisory community, a
maintainer of [pollinations/pollinations](https://github.com/pollinations/pollinations),
Hacktoberfest project administrator and student coordinator since 2024, GDG
on Campus organiser at JIS University in 2025–26 (21 events), and a mentor in
GirlScript Summer of Code 2025, OSCI 2025 and ESSOC 2026. See
[Mentoring experience](/about/#mentoring-experience).

## Are the commitments written down?

Yes. Every mentor and administrator signs a public commitment, published on
the [agreements page](/agreements/), and everyone follows the
[Code of Conduct](/code-of-conduct/).

## How can we contact the organisers?

Write to [gsoc@elixpo.com](mailto:gsoc@elixpo.com). The organisation
administrators and the programme coordinator read it; individual addresses
are on the [mentors page](/mentors/).
