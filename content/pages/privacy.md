## Who we are

This site, gsoc.elixpo.com, is published by Elixpo, an open-source
organisation, for its application to Google Summer of Code 2027. Questions
about this notice go to [gsoc@elixpo.com](mailto:gsoc@elixpo.com).

## This website

- **A static site.** Every page is a pre-built file. There are no accounts,
  no sign-in, no forms and no comment boxes on this site.
- **No cookies.** The site does not set cookies and does not store anything
  in your browser.
- **No analytics or tracking.** The site contains no analytics, advertising
  or tracking scripts, and no social media widgets.
- **Fonts and images are served from this site,** with one exception: the
  People page and each project idea page show mentors' public GitHub profile
  pictures, which your browser loads from GitHub. GitHub receives that
  request under the
  [GitHub General Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
- **Links to other sites,** such as GitHub and the Google Summer of Code
  site, take you to services with their own privacy policies.

## Hosting

The site is hosted on [Cloudflare Pages](https://pages.cloudflare.com/). To
deliver and protect the site, Cloudflare processes standard request
information as the host, such as your IP address, the page requested, the
time and your browser's user agent. Cloudflare handles this information
under the [Cloudflare Privacy Policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).

## Email

Messages to [gsoc@elixpo.com](mailto:gsoc@elixpo.com) are received by
Elixpo's organisers through Elixpo's mail provider. We use your message and
address only to answer you and to run the programme. We do not add you to a
mailing list and we do not share your message outside the people who need it
to reply.

## GitHub Discussions and issues

Questions in [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/orgs/elixpo/discussions)
(which open for GSoC 2027 once Google announces the programme dates) and issues in Elixpo repositories are public: anyone can read them. GitHub
operates these services under the
[GitHub General Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
Do not post private information there; write to
[gsoc@elixpo.com](mailto:gsoc@elixpo.com) instead.

## Google Summer of Code applications

You submit your Google Summer of Code application and proposal to Google,
through the [Google Summer of Code site](https://summerofcode.withgoogle.com/).
Google processes that information under the
[Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and the
[programme rules](https://summerofcode.withgoogle.com/rules).

During selection, Google makes your proposal and application details
available to Elixpo's organisation administrators and mentors. We keep this
information within the organisers and mentors, use it only to evaluate
applications and run the programme, and never publish it.

## Changes and questions

If this notice changes, we update this page and its effective date. Questions
about privacy go to [gsoc@elixpo.com](mailto:gsoc@elixpo.com).

**Effective date:** 29 October 2026.
